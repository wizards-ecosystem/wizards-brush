# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The Wizard's Brush should be a collaborative, technically rigorous project
where people can ask basic questions, share incomplete ideas, and disagree
without being demeaned. Participate in good faith, respect people's time and
privacy, and critique ideas and evidence rather than individuals.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, stalking, repeated disruption, and publishing another person's
private media or credentials are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, release channels, and project-related private communication. It also
applies when someone represents the project in another public space.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that
do not meet this standard. Responses may range from a private correction or
content removal to a temporary or permanent ban, based on impact, pattern, and
risk to the community.

Maintainers involved in a report must recuse themselves when they cannot review
it impartially. Reports and reporter identities will be shared only as needed
to investigate and act, subject to legal obligations.

## Reporting

Do not litigate conduct reports in a public issue. Contact a maintainer privately
using the contact method on their GitHub profile and include relevant links,
dates, context, and the outcome needed for immediate safety. If the report
concerns that maintainer, contact another project or organization maintainer.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
